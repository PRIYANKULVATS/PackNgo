About PackNgo

A unified AI-powered travel ecosystem built for modern, safety-conscious travellers.

What is PackNgo?

PackNgo is an integrated travel ecosystem combining a booking engine, AR/VR reviews, an AI personal travel agent, real-time safety and crowd tracking, and a social hub for solo travellers — all in one platform.

Vision & Mission

To make travel safer, smarter, and more personal using machine learning, augmented reality, and community-driven intelligence. Every traveller deserves a confident, protected, and personalized journey.

What We Provide

  • AI powered personal travel agent
  • Real-time crowd and safety tracking
  • AR and VR travel preview
  • Verified travel buddy network
  • Smart translator and cultural guide
  • Travel insurance and emergency mode
